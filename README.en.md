# MyDoughNut

An income and expense tracker for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

It is in TestFlight testing now. The App Store link will be added here after release.

## Features

**Entries**

- income and expenses
- categories, tags, and comments
- archived categories can be hidden

**Statistics**

- expenses, income, and the ratio between them
- reports by category, date, month, year, tag, and amount
- a calendar, period comparison, trends, averages, and 80/20
- short and long periods
- a balance overview with a pull-down gesture

**Currencies**

- a main currency and exchange rates
- an entry in another currency is converted into the main one

**Data**

- iCloud sync
- CSV import from iFinance 4

**Appearance**

- theme, income and expense colors, haptics
- the week starts on Monday or Sunday

The interface is available in Russian, English, Spanish, and Chinese. The system language can stay selected.
